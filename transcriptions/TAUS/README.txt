Explanation of folder and filenames:

taus_phon_with_informant_codes: Folder with phonetic transcriptions where informant codes are included. 
NB! Some recordings do not have phonetic transcriptions.  
taus_phon_without_informant_codes: Folder with phonetic transcriptions where informant codes are not included. 
NB! Some recordings do not have phonetic transcriptions. 
taus_with_informant_codes: Folder with orthographic transcriptions where informant codes are included.
taus_without_informant_codes: Folder with orthographic transcriptions where informant codes are not included.

a02.txt, taus_b03.txt, c5.txt, taus_b01-b02.txt
a02, taus_b03, c5 and so on are the original informant codes from the TAUS investigation. 
a refers to the A serie, b to the B serie and c to the C serie. 
Most of the transcriptions are interviews with one informant. 
In the B serie there are also conversations between to informants like in taus_b01-b02.txt.

For more information, go to: https://tekstlab.uio.no/nota/taus/english.html
Search the corpus in Glossa: https://tekstlab.uio.no/glossa3/taus3
