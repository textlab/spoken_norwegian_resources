CANS v.3.1 contains recordings and transcriptions from:
	USA: Seip and Selmer (1931) and Einar Haugen (1935 - 1942)
    USA: Arnstein Hjelde (1987 - 1992)
    USA and Canada: Janne Bondi Johannessen et al. (2010 - 2016)
    Swedish in USA: Maia Andréasson et al. (2011 - 2012)

Transcriptions from Latin America are searchable in CANS v.4, but the transcriptions are not available for download.

The developement of CANS has been ongoing since 2010 and has taken place within the framework of several projects.
The first transcriptions were written in Transcriber 1.5. These transcriptions are available for download in html format. 
There are two versions of each transcription: one phonetic and one orthographic. The two versions are written in seperate files.
The later transcriptions are written in ELAN and are available in txt format. The phonetic and orthographic versions are written in the same file.
