The transcriptions from NoTa-Oslo are available in two formats: html and txt. 

Explanation of filenames

181-182-c.txt  190-III-i.txt

Filenames comprise three parts: speaker 1, speaker 2, conversation/interview 

or

samtale_181-182.html  intervju_190-III.html


Speaker ids consisiting of digits are informants, while letters denote interviewers. c /samtale = conversation, i /intervju = interview
