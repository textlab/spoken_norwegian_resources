The transcriptions from LIA_NORSK are available in txt format. 

Explanation of filenames

aal_uio_02.txt aamot_ma_01-02

Filenames normally comprise three parts: location (kommune 1960), university owner, location file number 

The files from Maalførearkivet, UiO have informant numbers instead of file numbers, e.g. aamot_ma_01 and aamot_ma_02 


NTNU = NTNU - Norwegian University of Science and Technology
UiB = University of Bergen
UiO = University of Oslo
UiT = UiT, The Arctic University of Norway
ma = Målførearkivet, UiO



For more information, go to: https://tekstlab.uio.no/LIA/norsk/index_english.html
